// The 5 content angles. Format: name: what it means. Labelling and angle scoring both use these names.
story: a personal or client story with a turning point
contrarian: challenges what most people say about personal branding, including myth-busting
proof: shows a real result, number or before-and-after
how-to: a step-by-step framework, template or checklist
authority: a lens from your own field (for example a biotech mechanism) that others cannot copy
