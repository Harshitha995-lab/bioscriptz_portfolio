# Reference breakdown: "The final videos" (AI voice platform launch film)

**What the reference is.** A creator's comparison video. Two cuts of the same
product launch film for an Indian AI voice platform play side by side, one
labelled "Claude" and one labelled "Team". On-screen note: *"Same voiceover,
music and sound design. Only the visuals differ."* It ends with the creator
talking to camera (1:40). Length 1:44. Source frame 974 x 480.

**Why it matters for us.** It proves the model we want: **one voiceover and
music bed drive the edit, and the visuals are swappable.** That is exactly how
a reusable template should work.

## The style in one line
Dark cosmic purple-blue, frosted glass cards, huge kinetic words, and a new
visual every 1 to 2 seconds, all cut to the voiceover.

## Rhythm and retention

- **Cut rate:** a new shot or a major change roughly every **1 to 2 s**,
  faster than our first template (5 to 10 s per scene). Inside a shot something
  is always moving: text typing, cards rotating, waves pulsing.
- **Every on-screen word matches the voiceover** at the moment it is spoken
  ("Speak to everyone, their language" … "Accent" … "Ready").
- **Story order:** hook (speak everyone's language) → what it is ("India's most
  advanced AI voice platform") → how it works (type, choose a voice, generate)
  → proof (200+ voices, 25+ languages, many emotions) → features (record,
  transcribe, fine-tune, clone, music, noise removal) → who it's for (teacher,
  brand, anyone) → offer (lifetime access, no monthly fees) → CTA.
- **Repetition with variation:** the same scene type returns with new content
  (carousel with 4 languages, emotion word swapped 4 times). Viewers learn the
  pattern and keep watching for the next change.
- **Sound does half the work:** music hits and whooshes land on each cut. The
  template must be timed to the audio.

## What to borrow and what to avoid

**Borrow:** voiceover-first timing, 1 to 2 s pacing, glass cards, giant words,
the carousel, the stat pills, the emotion-word swap, the short-word CTA.

**Avoid copying:** the product's name, logo, exact layouts and the creator's
footage. We take the *techniques* and use each client's own brand, product
and words.

## What the template needs from us for each new video

1. **Voiceover** (recorded, or generated with an AI voice tool), plus music.
2. **Script with timestamps**, so every word lands on its line. With the audio,
   timestamps can be produced automatically.
3. **Brand:** colours, logo, fonts.
4. **Real assets:** product screenshots, photos of people (with permission),
   real numbers with sources.
5. **Hero images**, if wanted, made with an AI image tool or shot for real.
