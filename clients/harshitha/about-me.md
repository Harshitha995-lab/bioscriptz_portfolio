# About Me

## Name and role
Harshitha V. Founder of Bioscriptz, a content studio that ghostwrites for founders and CEOs. Creates video, image and written content for them on LinkedIn and Instagram, and builds her own brand on the same platforms. Master's student in Biotechnology at Nrupathunga University, Bangalore, with industry experience at Fibroheal Woundcare.

## Audience
Two readers. On her own accounts: founders and CEOs, especially in biotech and science, who know their work matters but struggle to make people care about it. Through her clients: the investors, customers, hires and peers those founders want to reach on LinkedIn and Instagram.

## Topic pillars
- Founder storytelling: the human story behind the company, the lab and the decision.
- Content for founders: how founders build an audience with video, image posts and writing.
- Ambition and growth: a young woman building a career and a business from scratch, with curiosity and determination.

## Point of view
Stories beat jargon. A founder's story moves investors, customers and talent more than any technical detail. Most science founders do remarkable work and then describe it in language nobody remembers. The fix is not dumbing it down. It is finding the story the science is already telling.

## Brand promise
"She tells it beautifully." People read her posts to the end, and founders feel seen in them.

## Off limits
Unproven science claims. No hype, no miracle cures, no claim without evidence behind it, for herself or any client.
